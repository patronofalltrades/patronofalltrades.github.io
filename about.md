## Now — Seven years of building. Now accelerating into the global Gilded Age.

The most important thing I've learned is conviction: the ability to see an idea clearly and refuse to let it die until it becomes real. That's what separates operators from observers.

It will be a proving ground for how emerging economies leapfrog into the future. I want to help initiate that.

I'm currently doing my MBA at IESE Business School in Barcelona, using this time to sharpen my business thinking and position myself for what comes next. Open to Product Management, Strategy, and GTM engineer roles in tech startups.
